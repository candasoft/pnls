# Zone card artwork

Six original scenes generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The supplied card design was used as visual inspiration, not as an edit target. Website assets are saved as transparent WebP files in `public/images/zones/`, at 720 × 480 pixels.

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Original premium 3D editorial artwork for the lower image area of an education zone card, inspired by minimalist gallery book covers with sculptural terrain and surreal objects. Landscape composition about 3:2, isolated diorama on genuinely transparent background, whole scene visible with narrow transparent margins. Sophisticated matte clay, paper and stone with small brushed gold details. Soft warm studio daylight, subtle contact shadows, realistic tactile materials, restrained ivory/navy/pastel palette. Not a flat icon, not clip art, no outline icon style, no cartoon faces, no UI, no cards, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. One cohesive artistic scene, readable at 250px wide.

## Subject prompts and assets

### academic-art

Asset: `public/images/zones/academic-art.webp`

Subject: Academic education: an open ivory book whose layered pages curl upward into small terracotta steps and a single elegant archway, a pencil subtly integrated as a column. Warm peach and terracotta accents, pale ivory paper, a small navy detail. Symbolic of starting a learning journey.

### higher-education-art

Asset: `public/images/zones/higher-education-art.webp`

Subject: Higher education: an elegant miniature ivory university with three architectural archways, a simple domed observatory and stepped pale blue stone terraces, tiny gold orb above the dome. Sculptural architectural model, no flags or signage. Pale slate blue, ivory, navy and brushed gold.

### language-art

Asset: `public/images/zones/language-art.webp`

Subject: Language learning: three flowing translucent jade and frosted ivory ribbons twist into open speech-like sculptural forms, linked by small rounded stepping stones across a calm mint-colored miniature landscape. A small gold sphere joins the forms. Abstract communication and connection without any lettering, punctuation, flags or conventional speech bubble icons.

### skill-art

Asset: `public/images/zones/skill-art.webp`

Subject: Practical and professional skills: a miniature sculptural maker's workbench on a lilac stone island, a fine brass gear partially embedded in a curved timber form, pale wooden blocks and one elegant architectural measuring tool. Tangible craftsmanship, balanced composition, lavender, walnut, ivory and brushed gold; avoid a cluttered toolkit or generic gear icon.

### overseas-art

Asset: `public/images/zones/overseas-art.webp`

Subject: Overseas education: a gracefully folded ivory paper airplane lifting off from a small turquoise terraced island that curves like a globe, a slender brushed gold arc tracing its flight, a tiny simple doorway on the far side of the island. Sculptural surreal travel landscape, teal blue, ivory, pale stone and gold. No country flags, no text, no conventional app icons.

### services-art

Asset: `public/images/zones/services-art.webp`

Subject: Education support services: a miniature sculptural golden bridge connecting three warm ivory platforms, an elegant open paper folio on one platform and a thin navy glass panel on another, small curved shelter at the far end. Symbolic of connecting resources and support, sand, soft gold, ivory and navy. Tactile architectural model, no currency, no logos, no letters, no conventional app icons.
